Company: Northwind Outdoor Co. (fictional sample)
Quarter: Q2 2026

Operator: Good afternoon and welcome to the Northwind Outdoor second quarter 2026 earnings call.

CEO: Thanks everyone for joining. This was a solid quarter. Revenue grew 6% to $412 million, and gross margin improved 80 basis points to 41.2%. Our direct-to-consumer channel had record sales, and we gained share in footwear. That said, wholesale demand was softer than we planned, and some retail partners are being cautious with orders.

CFO: Thank you. Operating income was $38 million, up from $34 million last year. Diluted EPS was $0.52. For the full year, we are maintaining our guidance of revenue between $1.70 billion and $1.75 billion. We expect gross margin of approximately 41%. Currency could be a headwind of roughly 1% to reported sales.

Question-and-Answer Session

Analyst (Jordan Lee, Example Securities): Can you talk about the wholesale softness? Is that a demand problem or just timing?

CEO: We believe it is mostly timing. Some partners may be holding less inventory because of macro uncertainty and weaker consumer spending in certain regions. We are cautious, but our sell-through data is still healthy.

Analyst (Priya Shah, Sample Capital): On margins, how much pressure are you seeing from promotions?

CFO: There is some pricing pressure in the market and competitors have been more promotional. We could see a little margin pressure in the back half, but we expect cost savings to offset most of it.

Analyst (Jordan Lee, Example Securities): Any update on tariffs?

CFO: Tariffs remain a risk. If new tariffs go into effect, we might need to adjust pricing. It is too early to say how large the impact could be.
